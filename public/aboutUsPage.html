About CityBikes

Our mission, goals, and how to use the app to find bikes anywhere in the world.

What Is CityBikes?

CityBikes is a web application that aggregates real-time data from bike-sharing networks around the world using the open CityBik.es API. Whether you are a daily commuter, a tourist exploring a new city, or a researcher studying urban mobility, CityBikes gives you access to live station data from hundreds of networks across more than 50 countries.

With CityBikes you can search for any city or country and immediately see a map of every bike station in that area, along with real-time availability — how many bikes are ready to rent and how many docks are free.

No account required. No downloads. Just search, find, and ride.

Open Data. Open Riding.

All data is sourced from the free, publicly available CityBik.es API — a community-maintained index of worldwide bike networks.

Our Goals

What we set out to achieve with this project.

Accessibility

Make bike-sharing information easy to find for everyone, regardless of city or country. No prior knowledge of local bike networks needed.

Global Reach

Support travelers and tourists who need quick information about bike infrastructure in unfamiliar cities around the world.

Real-Time Accuracy

Display live station data so users always know whether a station has bikes available or docks free before making a trip.

Sustainable Cities

Encourage eco-friendly urban transport by making bike-sharing more visible and convenient to use.

How to Use the App

Follow these steps to find available bikes at your destination.

1

Go to Location Search

Click Find Bikes in the navigation bar or the Search Now button to open the Location Search page.

2

Enter a City or Country

Type the name of a city (e.g., Washington D.C.) or country (e.g., France) into the search bar and press Search.

3

View Networks on the Map

Matching bike networks appear on an interactive world map. You will see the network name, city, country, and operator.

4

Select a Network

Click a network in the results list or on the map to open its Result Page with full station detail.

5

Check Station Availability

The Result Page shows each station pinned on the map with a live count of available bikes and free docks. Stations are color-coded by availability.

Tip: Station data refreshes each time you load the results page. Reload the page right before you head out for the most current availability.

Data Source

CityBikes is powered by the CityBik.es API, an open-source project that aggregates data from city bike networks worldwide. Two endpoints are used:

  • Networks Endpoint — Returns all known bike networks including their name, city, country, latitude, and longitude.
  • Station Endpoint — Uses a network's unique ID to fetch the live status of every station in that network, including available bikes and free docks.
