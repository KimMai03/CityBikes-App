FAQs


Why does my city not appear in the search?

Not every city has a bike-sharing program. Coverage depends on whether a network is registered with the CityBikes API. Try searching by country instead.

How often is station data updated?

Data is fetched live from the CityBikes API each time you load the results page. Reload the page for the most current availability.

What do the station colors on the map mean?

Green = bikes available   Amber = limited (3 or fewer)   Red = no bikes.
